### What is Shield Systems?

Shield Systems (Universal Endpoint Integrity Suite) is a defensive visibility platform. It helps authorised operators understand what is stored, running, and communicating on their own licensed devices and infrastructure — without decrypting content or bypassing platform security controls.

### What are the three Shields?

| Shield | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Endpoint Shield** | File integrity, entropy checks, hidden attributes, persistence inventory (Windows, macOS, Linux, mobile) |
| **Edge Shield** | Hypervisor / virtual-disk visibility, browser storage audits, shared-folder monitoring |
| **Data Shield** | Process-level network accounting, message/frame counts, path mapping, device identity attribution |

### Does Shield decrypt files or break encryption?

**No.** Shield does not decrypt, unlock, or bypass passwords, encryption, sandboxes, SIP, TCC, Scoped Storage, or any platform protection. It reports integrity and visibility signals only.

### Can I use it on devices I do not own or manage?

**No.** Use is limited to account holders and authorised operators of licensed devices. Unauthorised access violates the Acceptable Use Policy and applicable law.

### What is Family Link?

A visible guardian-link feature for families. The child always sees a banner, can unlink at any time, and location is shared only when Location Share is explicitly enabled. It is designed for safety, not covert monitoring.

### Does the Guide AI give attack or exploitation advice?

**No.** The public Guide endpoint is documented to refuse attack, exploitation, or bypass advice. It provides defensive playbooks only (everyday, elevated, stalking, child threat levels).

### Are scan results real?

Yes. Scan history endpoints return real results only. Nothing is invented or fabricated.

### What data does Shield collect?

Under an authorised account and licensed devices: account/plan metadata, device inventory & heartbeats, scan results, user-approved diagnostics, Family Link presence (location only if enabled), and consented support-session metadata. See the full Privacy Policy and the short Privacy Note.

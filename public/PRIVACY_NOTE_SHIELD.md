### What Shield is designed to handle

Under an authorised account and licensed devices, Shield may process:

- Account and plan metadata (profile, trial, add-ons, threat-level preference)  
- Device inventory and heartbeat data (OS, Shield version, last scan) you associate with the account  
- Scan history and integrity/telemetry results generated on licensed devices  
- Diagnostics **only when you approve** upload  
- Family Link presence (last seen; location only if Location Share is enabled)  
- Support session metadata when you create a session and Allow staff join  

### What it is not for

- Selling personal data for advertising  
- Collecting data from devices you do not control or are not authorised to manage  
- Silent diagnostics without the user-approved flows documented on the API  

### Your choices

- Revoke device licenses and unlink family links as provided in the product  
- Refuse diagnostic upload and support Allow

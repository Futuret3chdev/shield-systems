## 1. Purpose

Shield is a **defensive visibility** product. It is intended to help account holders and authorised operators understand:

- What is stored on licensed devices  
- What is running and persisting  
- What is communicating (process- and device-attributed where available)  

It is **not** an offensive toolkit and is not licensed for unauthorised access, interception, or bypass of security controls.

---

## 2. Acceptable use

You may use Shield Systems and the Shield API when:

- You are the account holder or an authorised operator for the licensed devices  
- Use is lawful in your jurisdiction and complies with platform terms  
- Family Link and similar features are used with appropriate consent and the visibility rules published on the product (e.g. banner always shown; child can unlink)  
- Diagnostics and support sessions are started only with user approval, as documented on the live API  

You must **not**:

- Use Shield to access devices or accounts you are not authorised to manage  
- Attempt to decrypt protected content, unlock passwords, or bypass sandboxes / OS protections  
- Request or rely on the Guide AI for attack, exploitation, or bypass advice (the public Guide endpoint is documented to refuse attack advice)  
- Misrepresent scan results or invent findings  

Violation may result in suspension of account, license, or API access, and may be reported where required by law.

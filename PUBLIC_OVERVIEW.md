## Why Shield Systems Exist

Modern environments are complex. Files can hide in alternate streams or high-entropy containers. Virtual machines and shared folders create new storage paths. Browsers and games maintain large local state. Network traffic can be attributed to processes — but only if you can see it.

Futuret3ch Security’s Shield Systems focus on **visibility and integrity**. The goal is not to break security boundaries; it is to give operators a clear, auditable picture of their own systems so they can spot the unexpected.

---

## The Three Shields

| Shield | What it does |
|--------|----------------|
| **Endpoint Shield** | File integrity, entropy checks, hidden attributes, and persistence inventory on Windows, macOS, Linux, and mobile. |
| **Edge Shield** | Hypervisor and virtual-disk visibility, browser storage audits, and shared-folder monitoring. |
| **Data Shield** | Process-level network byte accounting, message/frame counts, storage-path mapping, and device identity attribution. |

Together they form a layered view that feeds a central dashboard intended for the path **memetorrent.futuret3ch.com.au/shield**.

---

## Where It Looks

Coverage is designed across everyday and enterprise environments:

- Desktop and server operating systems (Windows, macOS, Linux)
- Mobile platforms (iOS and Android) within normal sandbox and MDM constraints
- Virtualisation layers (including awareness of virtual disks and nested environments)
- Major web browsers (local storage, service workers, extension activity where permitted)
- Common gaming client libraries (with coexistence rules for legitimate anti-cheat software)

---

## What Operators Gain

- A clearer inventory of files, directories, and persistence mechanisms that belong on a system.
- Signals when content looks anomalous (for example, high entropy behind a simple file extension).
- Attribution of network activity back to processes and stable device identities.
- Visibility into virtual-disk and shared-folder activity that traditional guest-level tools may miss.
- A single place to review findings under the Shield Systems umbrella.

---

## Clear Boundaries

Shield Systems and the Universal Endpoint Integrity Suite are built for **defensive visibility only**.

- They do **not** decrypt or unlock protected content.
- They do **not** bypass passwords, encryption, or platform sandboxes.
- They do **not** provide tools for unauthorised access to other people’s data.
- All use must comply with applicable law, organisational policy, and platform terms.
